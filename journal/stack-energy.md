# Where to draw the stack energy boundary

A stack calculation is a useful early engineering check, provided its boundary is explicit. In a series stack, current is common to every cell, voltage adds across the cells and the hydrogen production rates add. With identical cells and a fixed Faradaic efficiency, the DC specific energy follows directly from cell voltage and Faraday's law.

The number of cells changes total power and hydrogen output but cancels out of specific energy under those assumptions. That is a useful sanity check.

Real system energy consumption also includes conversion losses and auxiliary loads. A DC number is not a system efficiency figure, and a fixed cell voltage is an input rather than a prediction of performance.
