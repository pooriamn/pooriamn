Electrochemical engineering workbench

A few calculations to explore, using idealised models and synthetic inputs.

Impedance response

Change the three parameters of a series resistance and parallel RC element. The Nyquist plot updates on calculation.

Equal physical axis scales; ideal RC circuit only. Curve spans five decades either side of the characteristic frequency.

Battery charge throughput

Enter time in seconds and signed current in amperes, one comma-separated pair per line. Positive current means charge; negative means discharge.

Linear current interpolation, split at zero crossings. This reports charge throughput, not SOC or battery health.

Stack balance

Open the stack calculator for DC power, hydrogen output and specific energy.
